# Counting Points Modulo $p$
This project investigates the distribution of the number of points $N_p$ on elliptic curves over $\mathbb F_p$ for odd primes $p$ of good reduction. We derive a formula expressing $N_p$ in terms of a sum of Legendre symbols, and then study the Trace of Frobenius $a_p=p+1-N_p$. Specifically, we show that for elliptic curves of the form $E:y^2=x^3-n^2x$, that $a_p=0$ if and only if $p\equiv 3 \pmod 4$. Utilizing this formula, we compute $a_p$ of the elliptic curves $y^2=x^3-x+1$ and $y^2=x^3-x$ for primes $p<20000$ of good reduction, and plot the normalized values $a_p/2\sqrt p$ as histograms. We then observe that the plotted distributions resemble those of the Sato-Tate distribution $\tfrac{2}{\pi}\sqrt{1-t^2}$ and the CM Sato-Tate distribution  $\tfrac{1}{2\pi}\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-t^2}}+\tfrac{1}{2}\delta_0(t)$ respectively. Finally we introduce the notion of potential complex multiplication, its relation to the j-invariant, the theorems which explain our observation about the histograms we compute for the two elliptic curves, and we introduce the characterization of supersingular primes.
